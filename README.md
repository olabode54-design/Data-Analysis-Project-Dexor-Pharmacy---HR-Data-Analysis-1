# Data-Analysis-Project-Dexor-Pharmacy--HR-Data-Analysis-1
Staff attendance, Work-from-Home, and Sick leave analysis using Power BI.

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to track and analyze employee attendance patterns at Dexor Pharm Plc. The dashboard provides real-time insights into presence rates, work-from-home trends, and sick leave patterns to support workforce management and HR planning.

## Project Objective
· Monitor daily employee attendance and presence rates
· Track work-from-home (WFH) adoption across the organization
· Analyze sick leave (SL) patterns and trends
· Identify attendance patterns by day of week and time periods
· Support data-driven decisions for workforce optimization

## Key Performance Indicators
· Overall Presence Rate: 91.8% (Organization-wide attendance)
· Work From Home %: Remote work adoption rate (13.01% peak on Fridays)
· Sick Leave %: Sick leave utilization (5% peak in April 2022)

## Key Insights
Overall Attendance Health
· Strong organizational presence at 91.8%, indicating good workforce stability
· Only 8.2% absenteeism rate across the monitored period

Attendance Patterns by Day
· Highest attendance: Monday (93.21%) and Tuesday (93.03%)
· Lowest attendance: Friday (90.19%) - 3% drop from Monday peak
· Gradual decline: Attendance decreases as the week progresses

Remote Work Trends
· Friday is the most popular WFH day at 13.01%, nearly double the Tuesday rate (8.11%)
· Employees prefer remote work toward the week's end
· Some employees (Gregory Carr, Zaiden Wheeler) are 100% remote.

Sick Leave Patterns
· Peak sick leave: April 2022 (5%)
· Notable drop in May-June 2022 (0%), possibly due to seasonality or policy changes
· Individual variations: Ayanna Atkins (10.71% SL) requires attention

Individual Performance
· Top performers (lowest absenteeism): Milley Combs (83.93% presence, 0% SL, 0% WFH)
· Concerning cases:
  · Gregory Carr (52.38% presence) - Critical attention needed
  · Ayanna Atkins (58.93% presence) - High sick leave (10.71%)
  · 6 employees below 75% presence threshold

## Recommendations
1. Investigate low performers: Review cases of employees with <75% presence (Gregory Carr, Ayanna Atkins, etc.)
2. Wellness programs: Consider initiatives to reduce sick leave, especially for employees with >5% SL
3. Friday scheduling: Account for 13% remote workforce when planning Friday meetings/activities
4. Mid-week focus: Schedule critical meetings Tue-Wed when attendance peaks
